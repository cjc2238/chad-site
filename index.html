About

As Vice President of AI Innovation & Engineering at ZoomInfo (NASDAQ: GTM), I set the enterprise vision for data science and AI and turn it into products that ship. Working alongside C-suite leaders, I build teams and a culture where data-driven decisions are the default, and I direct the full lifecycle of new AI tools, from generative AI and automation to chatbots, that strengthen our core products and sharpen how the business runs.

Outside the company, I work on making AI more responsible. I am a founding member of New York University's Computational Logic Lab and an adjunct professor at both NYU and Columbia University, where I connect early research on how large language models reason about ethics to the problems businesses actually face. I hold a Ph.D. in Cognitive Science and Machine Learning from Columbia University.

I am active online and easy to reach on LinkedIn or by email.
